# BizPilot 90-Second Demo Script

Use the public `/demo` page for the opening, then switch to the real application for the authenticated workflow.

## 0–10 seconds — Opening

“BizPilot is a small-business management SaaS that brings customers, invoices, payments, expenses, and reporting into one workspace.”

Show the public demo dashboard and the four headline metrics.

## 10–25 seconds — Customers

“First, I can manage the people and companies the business works with.”

Open Customers and show search, add, edit, and archive actions.

## 25–45 seconds — Invoice

“From there, I can create an invoice using a customer and products or services.”

Create or open an invoice and show line items, tax, discount, total, and status.

Point out the PDF download action.

## 45–60 seconds — Payment

“Once the invoice is sent, I can record a payment and see the remaining balance.”

Show a partial payment and the reduced outstanding balance.

## 60–72 seconds — Expenses

“Business costs are tracked separately, so the dashboard can show what is actually left after expenses.”

Open Expenses and add or show an expense.

## 72–82 seconds — Reports

“Reports bring the important numbers together so the owner can quickly understand the business.”

Open the Reports view and show the key metrics/charts.

## 82–90 seconds — Closing

“BizPilot is built as a customizable starting point, so a developer or agency can adapt the foundation instead of rebuilding the basic business workflows from zero.”

End on the dashboard or sales page and show the source-code offer.

## Recording rules

- Use fictional data only.
- Do not display secrets or environment variables.
- Do not record real customer information.
- Keep the cursor movement simple.
- Prefer one continuous workflow over many unrelated screens.
- Mention that prices and licensing depend on the package being sold.
